CLIPS

CLIPS is a personal tool by Aniol that detects highlights in Twitch live streams, turns them into short vertical clips and uploads them to the owner’s own TikTok account through TikTok’s official APIs.

CLIPS és una eina personal d’Aniol que detecta moments destacats en directes de Twitch, crea clips verticals i els puja al seu propi compte de TikTok mitjançant les API oficials.
